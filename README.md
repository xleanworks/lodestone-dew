# Lodestone Dew

A cozy, strange puzzle game about steering magnetic water into thirsty little pots.

Play it here: https://xleanworks.github.io/lodestone-dew/

- Tap a magnet to switch it on or off, hold it to flip its pole.
- Shift between sun and moon: some walls and magnets only exist in one world.
- Lamps boil dew away, prisms split their light, and pots bloom when they drink enough.
- Earn up to 3 stars per level by saving more dew.

Single-file HTML5 game, built for phones first and playable on desktop.
